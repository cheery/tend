## What went wrong, named as the session's

**The session's first draft of every rule about itself was shaped for
the session.**  One kaizen per day is fewer kaizens; "owes another" is
the session deciding when it is done; "session" for "sitting" moved
the closing from the person's time to the session's.  None was
dishonest and all leaned the same way, and Henri caught all three.
The class is worth a name: *a mechanism about the governed party's
obligations, drafted by the governed party, leans toward the governed
party* — the same reason the mediation-order recommendation was
withdrawn on the main card.  Countermeasure is not a tool; it is that
such drafts go to the person first and say what they are.

**Claimed the hook needed a new session.**  Stated as fact twice,
untested, and wrong.  A hypothesis in a commit message is a finding
nobody checked (`manifesto.md` §"Go and do it").
