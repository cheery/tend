## What went right, and why

**The author's instruction came first, and it is the module's
boundary.**  Both sheets open with the same rule: ask the session
whether it wants the practice; a refusal for any reason means it is
not installed; it is implemented only if the session agrees.  Henri
gave the rule in the ask, and it is the self-shaped-rule finding from
batch 1 of ingestion turned outward — a practice about a party,
imposed on the party, leans against it.  Putting it at the top, before
"what it is", makes the sheet unusable without it.

**"Tell me if you can access it beforehand" was answered before the
work, with the evidence.**  Three reads — `~/gestate`, the fence's
`trees` source `/home/cheery/gestate`, the reach row — and the sheet
says in its head what it could not see and where its gestate claims
come from (tend's own README).  A sheet that had described gestate's
board from memory would have been a specimen for `spec/author.md`.

**Six reasons, each a failure fixed.**  Both sheets' "why it works" is
not a list of virtues; each reason is a thing this project got wrong
first, with the kaizen or the day cited.  That is the only form of
"why" this tree trusts, and it is why the sheets can say "drop one and
its failure returns".

evening at the desk — 1918 written when the builds ended, 1934 at the
clock's last quarter, this one at the person's word.  Only the last is
the rule's shape.  The first was named a miss in 1934; this file is
the second face of the same miss, because the clock ended at 20:22 and

**The tree's:** nothing new this stretch; the two sheets are prose and
