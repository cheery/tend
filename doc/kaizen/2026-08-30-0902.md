## What went right, and why

**The post was reviewed against the card, not summarised.**  Henri
asked whether Zechner's pi post changed my view of tools for sessions.
The answer was no on day one and yes on two things, and both landed
as card text the same sitting: the "never a shell" paragraph was
stronger than the tree's own evidence (the session is a shell under a
fence) and now says its because; and the compare.py measurement has a
prediction written before it runs, with a number to beat.  His
follow-up — "very short prompts and tool prompts … I'd like those to
be short in this project as well" — became a section with the tree's
own numbers beside the post's: lead.sh's prompt is 98 words, the talk
sends none.  A rule with a number is a gate the next session can
write; one without is a habit.

**Both defects were read before they were fixed, and both guesses
were wrong.**  F000's entry suspected the first window's slide; the
`$STATE/ticks` instrument (kaizen 1934 item 2, built first) showed
every odd tick — `date +%s` truncating, a 2 s window that was 1.02 s.
F001's entry suspected keep's startup or the wrong process; one run
under burners showed the CPU column *fall* — the program's own exit
between two ticks, read as no progress, written as idle, the exit
masked to 0 and the death notice lost.  That second one is
person-facing: a llama-server crash between ticks would have shown as
"idle" on the panel with no line.  Eight lines of ticks cost eight
seconds; the two guesses had cost three gate refusals.

**The fixture rule was paid a fifth time and recognised on sight.**
After F000's fix the burst test failed 20 of 20 under the shake, and
the ticks said the burster got 10 ticks a second: a program that
burns 0.3 s of *wall* time is 10 % of a core on a loaded box, and the
rule was right to call it idle.  Both launcher fixtures now burn CPU
time and say `idle 4`, and the gates for the two defects are their own
tests, each tried against HEAD's launcher before its green was
trusted: the `date` shim that skips 3 s a read (old: stopped at 2 s;
new: ran to its exit), and the exit-3-at-1.5 s (old: idle, rc 0, no
notice; new: `exited 3`, rc 3, the notice).  Shakes: 0 of 10 on all
four.

no-op, an assert that forbade my own sentence, a `\n` that became a

it was asserted on — the same fault as the `suspected` fields, one
level down.

**Henri's: none that I saw.**  The door line sat uncommitted by his
