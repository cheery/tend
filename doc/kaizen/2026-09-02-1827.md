# Kaizen — 2026-09-02, the 18:27 sitting: the danger is small, and he went to bed on it

*One file for one sitting, about 18:15 to his "kirjoita kaizen" at
18:29, one commit (`aaf5739`, 18:27) and this file.  He set the clock
to 60 minutes and closed at fourteen; the lamp said 49 left.*

**What it was.**  No build.  He came with a thought, in Finnish: he had
once asked a model what the likeliest danger in AI is, it said misuse,
and he had taken misuse to mean something large; now he sees it is
small and worldly — homework done for you, vanity fed, trust given too
far, a question at three in the morning — and that nothing rings for
things that size.  The session's answer was that an alarm sized to the
event misses everything under its threshold and a small thing is only
ever seen by a counter, which is the shape the tree already has: the
sitting clock, a kaizen at every sitting, the flake card's "each real
once counted".  He asked whether it was a memory or a card.  Neither,
the session said — a card needs a build and a memory is the session's,
not the tree's — and proposed `spec/author.md`; he said vision; the
session agreed and said why it was better: the line *decides* what
"dangerous to use" and "uhata turvallisuutta" are the size of, and
`vision.md`'s own rule is that a line earns its place by having decided
something.  Both went in at his word — his words verbatim in
`spec/author.md` §"What the danger is" with the session's reading kept
beside them, and one line in `vision.md` §"Tend as a working platform"
— and committed through the gate, 363 green.

**What went right.**  The question was answered before anything was
written, and the place was found by reading the tree's own rules for
its documents rather than by picking the nearest file: `vision.md`'s
header says what earns a line there, and that sentence settled it.  The
session changed its recommendation when his was better, and said so in
one line.  His uncommitted `env LD_LIBRARY_PATH=…` line in `llm/grant`
was left standing because he doubted it himself in the same message
that said "commit everything" — his doubt outranks his instruction when
they arrive together, and the answer he got was the trade (a path in
the grant versus an rpath in the binary), not a decision made for him.
And the sitting ended the way the vision line says: *"En kehtaisi
viettää iltaa tässä nyt kun olen niin hienosti tunnistanut asian mikä
siinä olisi väärin"* — having named the evening question as a small
misuse, he did not spend the evening here.  The rule was applied to its
author within the hour of its writing, which is the only test a line
like that gets.
