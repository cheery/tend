## What went right

**The card names a problem, not the fix.**  "A node is a program, not a
session, until it carries the cords" is a state of the tree, and the
three cords are named as what is absent, not as a design to adopt.  The
"what would make this card wrong" is real: if no node is ever a session,
the cords are the hosted session's alone — and the frame is exactly that
a local model *is* a session, so the hole is real the moment one leads
the work.

**It leans where it should.**  On `work-environment-ai`'s frame, on
`cords` (the hosted cords, andon blocked until 08-31), and on `keep`/
`resolver` (the grant half it completes) — each named, so a reader
finds the thread.
