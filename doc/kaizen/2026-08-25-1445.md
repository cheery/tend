## What went right

**The card names a problem, not its answer.**  The capability broker is
the obvious fix and it is written all over `work-environment-ai`; the
temptation was to card the fix.  The `because` names ambient authority
instead, and a whole section — "what it must not become" — exists to
keep the broker out of this card.  That is the board's first gate
working on the hardest case for it: a problem whose answer is already
half-designed elsewhere.

**The problem was measured, not asserted.**  Before writing the card,
one probe from inside the fence: a process reads the tree, the leash
ledger, the tree next door and another sitting's state, none of them
given.  The card opens with the table.  A problem card with a
measurement is a claim that can be checked, not a worry — and it found
the honest shape: the fence's win is real but directory-coarse, and
this problem is one bind below it, not beside it.

**The widening was taken at face value and folded in.**  "By anything
really" is a bigger claim than the Finnish line — it pulls sessions and
the environment into the same problem as programs.  The card's `because`
carries his words and the widening both, rather than quietly narrowing
back to "programs" because that is what `spec/os.md` happened to write.

the first architecture decision.  That reasoning is real, but ranking a
brand-new, unbuilt problem above observed, nearly-closed cards is a
judgement Henri may well undo — the note says so, but the session made
the call rather than leaving it flat for him.
