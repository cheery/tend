what are the memories — were answerable from the tree in one pass,
and the two that were genuinely his (who grades, whether to smoke
first) were asked as two picks rather than assumed.  Nothing in the
three arms changed afterwards; the fourth arm arrived from a
measurement, not from taste.

**Every fault was found by a mechanism, and none by attention.**  The
door's one-line refusal — built the previous evening for a 429 —
turned the very first grade into `temperature is deprecated for this
model` instead of three lines of JSON, and the courier had been
sending that knob to every wire since delivery began.  The hand gate
fired at 4 of 30 and refused to print a verdict.  The kaizen lamp
carried an owed file overnight.  The suite refused nothing today, and
that is the boring half of the same fact.

**The hand gate was not overridden, it was answered with a
measurement.**  Four disagreements, all one specimen type, both
readings defensible on the rubric as written.  Henri's call was
*report both*, and instead of arguing the reading we built the

showed the grader's reading was calibrated (62/31/57 against
60/31/59).  A dispute about judgment turned into an experiment
overnight-cheap, and the sensitivity in the sheet shows the verdict
holds either way.

**The prediction was falsified and is reported as such.**  The card's
number was written before the run; seat's incorrect came back

least welcome.  The two findings worth having — the transition

**His questions produced the sheet's two best sections.**  *"Does

and `proposals/` are asserted never to be tree parts, red the day
someone adds one.  *"The test does little to show what replaced the

conditioning bought.

## What went wrong, and whose

**Mine: an eyeball reported as a count.**  Reading the smoke I said
two of ten tooled turns had read the exam's card; the count, once
mechanised, said four of ten — and 69 of 300 over the run.  I had the
accounts in front of me and estimated instead of counting, on the
same morning I wrote `manifesto.md` §"Go and see", whose whole point
is that a fluent reading is not a measurement.  The section is three
commits older than the lapse.

**Mine: a test that copied the live environment instead of building
the side it means.**  `test_reach_allow.py` refused `~/.config` to
prove the secret-places check — and the suite runs fenced, where
`~/.config` is not there, so the refusal came back for the wrong
reason.  `board/README.md` §"What the days taught" already carries
this rule in its plainest form, paid for four times in August.  Fixed
by building a home the test owns; it should not have needed the
failure.

**Mine: a parser copied without being checked.**  `simpleqa.py`'s
record reader kept the `V:` line's `door model` whole as the model
name — copied from `compare.py`, where I had written the same defect
the day before and no assertion had pinned it.  The test caught it in
seconds, but it reached a second file first.

**Mine: a debt created in the shell.**  The transition matrix, the
provenance count and the looked-then-asserted split were computed
with ad-hoc shell for the sheet, not in `tools/simpleqa.py`.  The
sheet's numbers are therefore not reproducible by running the tool —
the next run would need me again.  Named in the sheet's §"What
follows", which is where a debt goes when it is not paid the same
day, but it was avoidable: `tally` gained the exam-card count as a
tool change and the other three could have gone in beside it.
