## What went right

**The card said where to go and the session went there.**  `session-
program`'s §11:10 ended with one sentence — "a node that leads work is
these three under a loop with the cords" — and the whole build fit in a
15-minute sitting because the bricks were already shaped as tools a
person runs: lead is forty lines of glue and two decisions.  The two
decisions are the ones that matter and both are the tree's own rule:
*the model's word is not trusted for the card* (an invented name is a
cord pull, not a proposal on nothing), and *a reply with no shape is a
cord pull* — the node that cannot say what it picked is the node that
should ask.  Neither needed new machinery; the andon record and
`propose.sh`'s boundary were already there.

**The lamp reopened by evidence, not by wanting it.**  On 2026-08-27 the
lamp was deferred with a measurement — a curl-answering node has
nothing to reflect.  Today a led turn does, and the account is written
by the turn, not by the model's will: a turn that pulls the cord or
fails to draft still writes it.  That is the same shape as the hosted
lamp (a side effect of the mechanism, not an act the session can
skip), which is what made porting it honest.
