## Why it is an F-number and not a card

The tree's own rule is that **a cap is a gate, and a gate says what it
stopped** — the executor's `readchars` cut prints `[… cut at N chars, at
line L of T; read(path, line=L) continues]`, and `F008` was resolved by
giving the digest's outer cap the same manners.  This one has none.  It
is not that eight lines is the wrong number; it is that a `because`
which stops mid-sentence reads to the mind as *a card whose problem
statement ends there*, and the mind has no way to know otherwise.  A
`because` names the problem the card exists for; a truncated one names a
different, smaller problem.

## What is not yet decided, and is the person's

Lifting the cut is now affordable and was not before: with `F008`'s cap
at 20000 characters, the whole board with every `because` entire is
**12088 characters, 60 % of the budget** (measured 2026-09-01), against
7516 as it stands.  So the shapes are:

- **say what was cut** — `[… 14 more lines]` on the card, the smallest
  and the one that matches what the tree already does elsewhere;
- **lift it** — carry each `because` whole while the board fits, and
  fall back to the outer cap's card-boundary drop when it does not;
- **leave it** — with a reason written down: a `because` may be
  deliberately more than a small mind should read at a pick, in which
  case the eight lines are a *summary* and should be named as one rather
  than left looking like an accident.

The third is a real answer and is why this entry does not pick.  What
would make the entry wrong is the same thing that would make `F008`
wrong: if the led loop's digest becomes dead code because a tooled turn
replaces it (`card:tools.md` day two), this is a note in its obituary.
