## What it cost, and what it did not settle

The turn was taken for a minute as "gemma4 emits no tool call" — the
`waits on` question on the card.  It says no such thing: a mind that
spends its whole cap thinking in the content channel never reaches the
line where it would call a tool or answer.  `calls: 0` and `picked
questions.md` are both artefacts of the missing knob and the first-line
parser, and the card's question stands open.

Two things the same turn did show, and they are real: gemma4 through
the door reads the digest and reasons over every card by name (the
board's digest arrived whole, which is what F008 and F009 were for);
and its reasoning wanted `kaizen-ingestion.md`, not the card the
account says.

## The fix's shape, not taken yet

Smallest: in `deliver.sh`, send the template knob when the url is the
node's own port whatever the model line says, since the node's wire is
the only one that reads it; and make `_thinking_line` say "not asked,
and the request carried no knob" when that is what happened.  Better
and slower: a door word for it, read by `door.sh --tools` beside
`temperature`, so a door says what its side accepts and the code stops
guessing from the url.  The second is a vocabulary change and a card's
conversation; the first is an evening.
