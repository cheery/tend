Two instances of one defect, in one function, four lines apart.  The
fix is the same for both: the big things ride in files —
`jq --slurpfile` for the conversation and the round's tool messages,
`jq --rawfile` for the assistant's own text, and `curl --data-binary
@file` for the request.  Nothing about the sizes changed; only how
they reach a process.

**The gate found the second instance.**  The first fix made the `jq`
error disappear and the test still failed — `the openrouter door did
not answer` — because `curl -d "$_body"` is the same cap one line
later.  A fix trusted on the disappearance of its first symptom would
have shipped half a repair, and this is `manifesto.md` §"The three
ways an instrument fails" in its second form: the check that has only
ever passed is a claim, and the check that has only ever passed *once
you fixed the thing you were looking at* is the same claim wearing a
green tick.

**Why it took until now.**  `readchars` was `12000` (gemma's number)
until 2026-08-30 evening, and 8 calls × 12 k stays under 128 k; the
number went to `60000` that night on a measurement of card sizes, and
`calls` to `16` the next morning, which put the cap two reads away.
The 600 SimpleQA turns of 2026-08-31 never reached it — their answers
are not in the tree, so their greps returned little and their reads
were few.  The first turn to read two real cards was the first paired
turn of the measurement this defect interrupted.
