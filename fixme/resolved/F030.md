The noise is the smaller half.  The larger is 3.19: the implicit
default becomes an error, and keep — every node's boundary — would fail
to start on the Python that makes it one.  Not fixed by the session that
opened this: keep is the enforcement boundary, and a change to it is his
to call.

**Fixed in the tree, 2026-09-24, at his "fix F030".**  Measured on
/usr/bin/python3 3.14.4 with the warning as an error: no `_layout_`
warns; `"gcc-sysv"` refuses `_pack_` outright ("_pack_ is not
compatible with gcc-sysv layout"); `"ms"` loads, 12 bytes, `parent_fd`
at 8 — the layout keep already had, now named.  keep.py carries
`_layout_ = "ms"`.  Gate, red first on the warning:
`test_keep.py::test_the_landlock_path_struct_is_the_kernels_twelve_bytes_and_loads_without_a_warning`,
