## Why the refusal is a `C:` line, which is not obviously wrong

The record's rule is that **the person watches the model act**
(`card:tools.md` §"Day one"): every call is a line, as it happens.  A
call the model made and the courier refused *is* something the model
did, and hiding it would make the record say the model stopped asking
when it did not.  The head arm asked for `board/questions.md` four times
past its budget; that is a fact about the turn worth keeping.

So the defect is not that the refusal is recorded.  It is that **one
number is being asked to mean two things** — what the model wanted, and
what the tree paid for — and every reader so far has taken it as the
second.

## The shapes a fix could take, none picked

- **(a) Two numbers.**  `calls  16 run, 4 refused` when any were
  refused, and `calls  9` when none were.  Cheapest, reads correctly at a
  glance, and the tally greps stay one field.
- **(b) A cap line.**  Keep `calls` as attempts and add `cap  16 —
  reached` beside it.  Says more about the turn, and leaves the
  misreadable field exactly where it is, which is the objection.
- **(c) Count only what ran, and put the refusals in their own line.**
  Truest to what a reader wants, and it silently changes the meaning of
  a field that appears in every account written so far — so old and new
  accounts would not be comparable without knowing which side of the fix
  they fell on.

(a) is the one that does not make the existing accounts unreadable,
because a bare `calls  9` still means what it always meant and the two
part form only appears where the old form was wrong.

## What would make this entry wrong

If no one ever compares call counts across arms.  The field has existed
since 2026-08-30 and was read carelessly for the first time on
2026-09-01, within an hour of there being two arms to compare — which is
the argument the other way.

## The count it already cost

One published comparison, corrected the same hour, in `card:tools.md`.
The correction is beside the original there rather than replacing it.
