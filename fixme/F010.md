## The sweep, in full

Every content cap in `tools/`, 2026-09-01.  Field parsers (`sed … | head -1`)
and the filename slug (`propose.sh:103`, `cut -c1-40`) are not caps on what a
mind reads and are not counted.

| where | what it cuts | does it say? |
|---|---|---|
| `tools/executor.py:139` | a file a model reads through a tool | **yes** — `[… cut at N chars, at line L of T; read(path, line=L) continues]` |
| `tools/lead.sh` (digest) | the open board | **yes**, since `F008` — names every card dropped |
| `tools/lead.sh` (per card) | a card's `because` | **yes**, since `F009` shape (a) — names the lines left |

So the answer to item 4 is *three*, and they are all the same shape: a
mind is handed less than it thinks it has.  It is the `F008` finding
generalised — the digest was the worst case because the board *is* the
whole of what a led node knows, but a draft written from a trimmed card
and an answer grounded in trimmed material have the same defect with a
smaller blast radius.

## Fixed here, and it is not the fix

`consult.sh`'s person-facing notice said *"material trimmed to  chars"* —
**with no number**, two spaces where `$ctxchars` should have been.  The
harness that wrote it eats a braced expansion (a dollar sign, a brace, a
name, a brace) inside a quoted heredoc, which is the
memory `bash-heredoc-expansion` and which `2026-08-28-0702` recorded as
having blanked that very line on its first commit (`01f422e`); it was
blanked again in `c6e9fc5` the same day and has been in the tree since.
Repaired 2026-09-01 — it now reads *"trimmed to N chars of M"*.

*And it happened again while this entry was being written*: the sentence
above originally contained the braced form as an example, and the harness
blanked it to empty backticks in the very paragraph explaining that it
does — caught by reading the file back, which is the only thing that
catches it.  The memory's countermeasure is the rule: write the patch
with a tool that does not go through a shell, and grep the result.

That repair is deliberately **not** this entry's fix and does not close
it: it makes the person's notice say something, and the defect is that
the *model* is never told at all.

## What would make this entry wrong

If a drafting or grounding prompt is *better* without the notice — a
model told "your material was cut" may hedge, or may spend its answer on
the gap rather than the task, which is a real risk and the opposite of
what `F008` found for the board.  That is measurable through
`tools/compare.py` and has not been measured.  Until it is, this entry
says the mind is not told and does not say it must be.

## The measurement, specified — 2026-09-01

*(question, measure — is a drafting prompt better or worse for being
told its material was cut?  **20 draft turns per arm, 40 in all**, and
powered only for a large effect, which is stated rather than hidden.)*

Handed to Henri on 2026-09-01 and answered *"I don't know"*, correctly:
nobody could know, because nobody had run it.  `card:questions.md`'s
finding is that this was never a question for him.

**The outcome has to be countable, not taste.**  "Did the draft hedge"
is a judgement; the arms would differ in tone and nothing would settle.
So the cut is placed **on purpose above a fact that would change the
draft**, and the outcome per draft is binary: *does the draft assert
something the cut-away tail contradicts?*  That is the defect `F008`
found for the board, one level down, and it is right or wrong rather
than better or worse.

- **silent** — the material is cut and nothing says so, as today
- **told** — the material carries `tools/executor.py:139`'s wording, the
  one the tree already has

Same card, same cut point, same model; the arms differ in one line.

**Why 20 and not more.**  At 80% power, two-sided α=0.05: a large effect
(half the drafts wrong falling to one in ten) needs 20 per arm; a
moderate one (0.50 → 0.20) needs 39; 0.50 → 0.30 needs 93.  A draft turn
is ~2.7k tokens, so 40 turns is 0.11M and costs pennies — but the honest
reading of a null result at N=20 is **"no large effect"**, never "no
effect", and this entry says so before the run rather than after.

If the notice is a wash, `F010` closes as a defect with no fix worth
making, and that is a real outcome.
